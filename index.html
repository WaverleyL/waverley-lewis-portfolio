Philosophy

I believe design is always more than aesthetics; it’s a way to shape meaning and craft narratives that people can resonate with.

I’m a student studying Interactive Arts + Technology at Simon Fraser University. I have experience across a range of design areas, grounded in a focus on communication and storytelling. I’m passionate about creating user-centered, research-driven, and accessible design systems that create memorable and meaningful experiences. Alongside my work, I’m interested in exploring artificial intelligence and how it can be used to enhance and evolve the role of design.
